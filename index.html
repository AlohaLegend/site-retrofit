07

Common questions

Do you need to replace our entire website?+

Not always. We start with what is already working, preserve valuable content and search equity, and rebuild only what is holding the site back.

How much time will this take from our team?+

Usually a short kickoff, one content review, and one consolidated revision. We handle structure, copy shaping, design, development, mobile refinement, and launch.

Can you work with our existing domain?+

Yes. Your company keeps ownership of its domain and accounts. We can work with the current setup or move the site to a cleaner one when that is the safer choice.

What happens after launch?+

You can take the finished site or keep us involved through Retrofit Care for hosting coordination, updates, monitoring, and ongoing improvements.
